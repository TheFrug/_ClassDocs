Restraint System

Each character has a Restraint bool the player can toggle as a free action.
If On => Defeating enemies leaves them incapacitated and vulnerable to looting
If Off => Defeating enemies kills them and makes looting them guaranteed

Unit Records show:
Wins
Losses
Kills (in red)

Benefits of system:
- Allowing players to role-play how they deal with enemies, such as by killing members of evil factions rather than leaving them alive
- Incap or Killing enemies results in different faction results
- Characters in army can interact with it differently, some can refuse to kill, others can have their endings change with enough kills
