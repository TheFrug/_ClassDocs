Restaurant gains 'Favor' with each successful service. The more customers are satisfied, the better.
- High price, high quality food will yield big bursts of Favor from wealthy patrons, but not from common people who can't afford the door price
- Free food can feed many, but will chew through ingredients. Gains massive favor and guarantees reputation gain with anyone who will eat the food.
